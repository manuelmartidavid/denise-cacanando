SCROLL TIMELINE

One master ScrollTrigger timeline, seven labels: hero · about · g1 · g2 · g3 · g4 · contact. Gallery scenes pin (320vh, 220vh, 240vh, 260vh — proportional to 24 / 7 / 7 / 12) with scrub:1; inside each, rotation maps to progress with snap:1/n and thumbs counter-rotate by −rotation. Between scenes the ring collapses to a seed while the flock's MotionPath owns the gap. Scene 03 swaps rotation for an x-translate track. ScrollTrigger.snap on labels so you can't stall mid-flight. Lenis for smoothing; single ScrollTrigger.refresh() after fonts load.

R3F STAGE

One fixed canvas behind the DOM, three systems on a shared scroll uniform: (1) pollen/petal points, ~4k, additive, half density over cream; (2) ~1,200 instanced butterflies, wing phase in the vertex shader, per-scene attractor targets; (3) the centre slot — a plane with a ripple displacement that settles on snap. No 3D eggs in the ring: ring thumbs are flat circular crops, and the orbitable ovoid is mounted only on the Ovalese detail route — one geometry, one material, seven 2K maps loaded on demand. That answers the perf worry without losing the 3D. Everything off-centre stays DOM, so copy is selectable and the LCP is an image, not a canvas. KTX2 + 1024px max textures, lazy per scene.

GUARDRAILS

prefers-reduced-motion: rings become snap-scroll lists, flock freezes into a static frieze, pins release — nothing structural is lost. Under 900px the ring unrolls into the horizontal snap list shown above and the rail becomes a bottom ticker. Mobile drops the butterfly system and keeps pollen at 25%. dpr capped [1, 1.75]; canvas paused when out of view. Real content, real alt text, and headings in DOM order — the scroll is decoration, not structure.

DATA & ROUTES

No CMS: one typed data module per category (artworks.ts · ovalese.ts · murals.ts · merch.ts) with slug, title, medium, size, year, status, images — the ring reads length from it, so counts are never hardcoded in the view. React Router: / is the scroll page, /:category/:slug is a leaf. Leaves are real routes, not modals, so they're linkable and shareable; entering one unmounts the gallery canvas and restores scroll position on back. Merch chips filter by kind (jackets 5, bags 4, shirts 2, earrings 1).
