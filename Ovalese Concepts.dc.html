2Resolving 1c vs 1d — and the hybrid that ends the argument

The two concepts answer different questions, which is why the choice feels hard. 1c solves transition — how you get from category to category. 1d solves browsing — how you move through pieces inside one category. So 2a and 2b push each to its honest best, and 2c gives each job to the mechanism that's actually good at it: flock between scenes, ring within a scene. My recommendation is 2c.

STAGE — R3F

One fixed canvas, three systems sharing a scroll uniform: (1) petal/pollen point field, always on, half density on the cream side; (2) ~1,200 instanced butterflies — half of 2a, since they only work the gaps; (3) the centre slot as a plane with a ripple displacement that settles on snap, and real ovoid geometry for the eggs.
